# Designing frictionless contact flows

Contact is a conversion. We remove friction with pre-filled WhatsApp links, progressive forms, and clarity on response times.

## Moves we make

- Place social proof near CTAs
- Make defaults helpful (subject, context)
- Keep microcopy confident and direct

These shifts increase form completion and keep conversations high-intent.
